About Miyawaki Forest@IITPkd

What is the Miyawaki Method?

The Miyawaki Method is an afforestation technique pioneered by Japanese botanist Dr. Akira Miyawaki. It involves planting native species close together to create dense, fast-growing, and biodiverse forests. This method is highly effective for restoring degraded land and increasing urban green cover.

Our Mission

At IIT Palakkad, our mission is to create a thriving Miyawaki forest that serves as a living laboratory for ecological research, student learning, and community engagement. We aim to promote biodiversity, enhance carbon sequestration, and inspire sustainable practices.

Key Features

  • 🌳 Dense plantation of native species
  • 🌱 Rapid growth and self-sustaining ecosystem
  • 🌏 Enhanced biodiversity and carbon capture
  • 🏫 Educational and research opportunities

🌱 Our Mission

To create a thriving Miyawaki forest at IIT Palakkad that serves as a model for ecological restoration and environmental education.

🌳 Our Vision

To inspire students, researchers, and the community to adopt sustainable practices and contribute to a greener planet.

🧑‍🔬 Student Involvement

The Miyawaki forest is also an interactive learning space. Students participate in the following activities:

  • 🌱 Tree height & girth measurements
  • 🌳 Species monitoring
  • 📊 Data collection for biomass estimation
  • 🌿 Research on native flora and climate adaptation
